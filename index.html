Read all property FAQs ↗
Which developers and projects can I explore?

Explore SOBHA homes and plots, Puravankara’s BluBelle, Meraki and Atmosphere, Provident’s Botanico, Ecopolitan, Deansgate and Sunworth City, and DNR Arista by DNR Group. Compare locations, configurations, guide pricing and timelines on the property listing page.

What apartment configurations are listed?

The collection includes 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK homes, plus 3.5 BHK residences at SOBHA Insignia. Configurations differ by project. SOBHA Townpark’s 1 BHK homes are marked sold out in the guide; current availability must be confirmed.

Are the prices on this website all-inclusive?

Not all prices are all-inclusive. Each project labels its pricing basis. Basic prices can exclude additional charges, and the guide indicates an additional ₹10–20 lakh for some projects. Request the latest itemised cost sheet for the exact configuration before making a decision.

Which projects offer residential plots?

SOBHA Sacred Grove at Chikka Tirupati and SOBHA Chartered Birdsong off Rajanakunte offer residential plots. Sacred Grove lists 1,200–1,650 sq ft plots and odd sizes; Chartered Birdsong lists 1,200–3,000 sq ft plots and odd sizes. Guide rates are expressed per square foot rather than as a total plot price.
