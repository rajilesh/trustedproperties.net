Separate Sarjapur Road from nearby addresses

SOBHA Neopolis is listed on Silver Oaks Main Road off Panathur Road, SOBHA Ayana on Panathur Road, and DNR Arista on Bellandur Main Road along the Marathahalli–Sarjapur Outer Ring Road corridor. Use the exact entrance location to compare these projects. A nearby neighbourhood name is not a substitute for a journey test.

Match the layout to the household

The guide lists 3 and 4 BHK homes at Altair, 1, 3 and 4 BHK homes at Neopolis, 3 BHK homes at Ayana, and 2, 3 and 4 BHK homes at DNR Arista. Compare size definitions, the floor plan and current inventory before treating a configuration as an available option. DNR pricing is on request in this catalogue.

Check the approach roads at your usual travel time, the practical route to your workplace and access to daily essentials.
