## Compare the exact residence

A shared neighbourhood and BHK label do not mean identical layouts. Look at bedroom dimensions, storage, balconies, workspaces and the usable area definitions in each floor plan. Compare the specific configuration and floor being quoted rather than the project starting price alone.

## Check the surroundings in person

Visit the project entrance and surrounding streets to assess everyday access, traffic, shops and the routes you expect to use. A location described near a lake does not establish a guaranteed view from a unit. Ask about the applicable site plans and inspect the actual orientation for any view-related decision.

## Ask for a complete quotation

Use the source-linked guides below for initial research, then request the latest itemised cost sheet, phase information and current inventory. Prices, specifications and availability should be checked for the particular home. The buying checklist helps you organise those questions before a booking decision.
