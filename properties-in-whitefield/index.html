The Whitefield label covers more than one location in this collection. Do not assume that an Old Madras Road address and a Soukya Road address share the same commute or station access. Request a location pin for the project entrance, then visit the approach roads and test your workplace route.

The SOBHA One World guide lists 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK homes, while Provident Botanico lists 2 and 3 BHK homes. Check each project page for its own sizes and pricing basis.
