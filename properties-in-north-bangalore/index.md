## Apartment, villament or residential plot?

Atmosphere and Ecopolitan are apartment projects in the catalogue. Deansgate lists 3 BHK villaments with garden and terrace options described in its guide. Chartered Birdsong is a plotted development. Comparing a plot price per square foot with an apartment total price will not give an equivalent budget; compare the applicable costs and intended use for each.

## Check phase and source before shortlisting

Completion information in a brochure can vary by phase. Review the individual source, ask which phase your quotation covers and request current availability. For a plot, discuss the applicable layout, infrastructure and building conditions; for a home, review the exact floor plan, size definitions and handover specifications.
