# Property FAQs

## Which SOBHA projects can I explore in Bengaluru?

The collection includes SOBHA One World, Altair, Neopolis, Ayana, Infinia, Sacred Grove, Chartered Birdsong, Insignia, Townpark and Magnus. Each project page lists its location, configurations, size range, indicative pricing and completion information from the supplied project guide.

## What apartment configurations are listed?

The collection includes 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK homes, plus 3.5 BHK residences at SOBHA Insignia. Configurations differ by project. SOBHA Townpark’s 1 BHK homes are marked sold out in the guide; current availability must be confirmed.

## Are the prices on this website all-inclusive?

Not all prices are all-inclusive. Each project labels its pricing basis. Basic prices can exclude additional charges, and the guide indicates an additional ₹10–20 lakh for some projects. Request the latest itemised cost sheet for the exact configuration before making a decision.

## Which projects offer residential plots?

SOBHA Sacred Grove at Chikka Tirupati and SOBHA Chartered Birdsong off Rajanakunte offer residential plots. Sacred Grove lists 1,200–1,650 sq ft plots and odd sizes; Chartered Birdsong lists 1,200–3,000 sq ft plots and odd sizes. Guide rates are expressed per square foot rather than as a total plot price.

## Are any homes listed as ready to move?

SOBHA Insignia is marked ready to move in the supplied guide. Other projects have completion timelines that vary by project and phase. Confirm current inventory, completion documentation and the specific phase directly with the developer.

## How do I request pricing or a site visit?

Use the enquiry form with your name, Indian mobile number, preferred configuration and budget, and authorise contact about your enquiry. Email is optional. A project’s enquiry link preselects a supported configuration. Mention your chosen project when the representative contacts you.

## Is Trusted Properties the official SOBHA website?

No. This is the Trusted Properties website. Project information is drawn from a supplied SOBHA project guide. Verify current specifications, approvals, RERA details, availability and commercial terms with the developer.

## Do the images and videos show the exact home I can buy?

The images and films are supplied reference visuals and artist’s impressions. They are not a promise of a particular unit, view, amenity or finish. Features differ by project; confirm the specifications and applicable plans for your chosen home.
