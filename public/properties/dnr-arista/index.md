## Prices and configurations

The supplied brochure describes DNR Arista at Bellandur Main Road, Marathahalli–Sarjapur Outer Ring Road: 4.5 acres, 417 flats across 8 blocks, 2B + S + 13 floors, and 30,000 sq ft of clubhouse and amenities. Its project profile lists 2 BHK at 1,260 sq ft, 3 BHK at 1,665–1,685 sq ft, and 4 BHK at 2,025 and 2,385 sq ft.
