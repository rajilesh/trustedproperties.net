# Residential plots in Bangalore

Canonical page: https://www.trustedproperties.net/residential-plots-in-bangalore/

Explore the two plotted communities in the catalogue: SOBHA Sacred Grove at Chikka Tirupati and SOBHA Chartered Birdsong off Rajanakunte. The source guides quote plot rates per square foot, rather than an all-inclusive price for every plot.

## Compare plot sizes and locations

Sacred Grove lists 1,200–1,650 sq ft plots plus odd sizes. Chartered Birdsong lists 1,200–3,000 sq ft plots plus odd sizes. Check the exact dimensions, plot number and layout position in the current offer. These guide ranges do not confirm which plots remain available.

## Turn a quoted rate into a useful budget

Ask for the area and rate applicable to the particular plot, then request the complete payable cost and a separate explanation of other charges. A per-square-foot guide rate is not the total purchase cost. Consider the future building budget separately if your plan is to construct a home.

## Review the applicable documents

Ask for the approved layout, access and infrastructure information, the specific project phase and the documents needed to check the plot. Use the official Karnataka RERA portal for the corresponding project record. Arrange independent professional review of the title and transaction documents before relying on them; a listing on this website is not a certification.
