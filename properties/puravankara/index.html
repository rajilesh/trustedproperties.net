About Puravankara and its Bangalore projects

Puravankara develops the Purva apartment projects listed here. Explore homes near Manyata Business Park, on Magadi Main Road, in HSR Layout, near Lalbagh, and along the Electronic City and Kudlu Gate corridors.

How do I choose the best Puravankara flat for me?

Start with your preferred neighbourhood, budget, BHK and move-in date. Compare floor areas and guide prices, then ask for the unit-specific floor plan and total cost sheet.

Where can I check ready-to-move, ongoing and upcoming Puravankara projects?

The supplied project sheets show guide possession dates, including a historical date for Purva Atmosphere. Ask Puravankara for current inventory, completion documents and details of ongoing or upcoming projects.

How do I book a Puravankara apartment?

Start with an availability or site visit enquiry. Before booking with the developer, request the current cost sheet, payment schedule, applicable Karnataka RERA details and booking terms for the unit or plot.
